# QoreChain Notes

Research and operational notes about QoreChain, light node participation, validator infrastructure, and post-quantum blockchain design.

## Core Ideas

- Post-quantum security for long-term cryptographic resilience
- Multi-VM architecture for broader developer compatibility

## Infrastructure Observations

QoreChain appears focused on long-term cryptographic resilience and infrastructure participation rather than short-term speculation. From an operator perspective, the most important areas to document are setup reliability, node uptime, resource requirements, and clear troubleshooting paths.
