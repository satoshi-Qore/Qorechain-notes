# Light Node Economics

## Why Does a QoreChain Light Node Require 1000 QOR?

One of the most common questions from new participants is why a QoreChain light node requires a stake of 1000 QOR.

The answer lies in network security and operator alignment.

---

## What Is a Light Node?

A QoreChain light node is not a validator.

It does not produce blocks or participate directly in consensus.

Instead, a light node contributes to the network by:

* Relaying network traffic
* Serving light client requests
* Improving network availability
* Contributing uptime and infrastructure capacity
* Participating in fee distribution

According to the current network design, light nodes receive a share of network fees for their contribution.

---

## Is the 1000 QOR a Fee?

No.

The 1000 QOR requirement is a stake, not a payment.

The stake remains locked while the node is operating and can be recovered when the operator decides to shut down the node.

This means participation does not permanently consume the tokens.

---

## Security: Sybil Resistance

The first purpose of the stake is to make Sybil attacks expensive.

A malicious actor attempting to launch a large number of fake light nodes must lock a significant amount of capital.

Example:

* 1 node = 1,000 QOR
* 100 nodes = 100,000 QOR
* 1,000 nodes = 1,000,000 QOR

The larger the attack, the more capital becomes locked and unavailable elsewhere.

This creates economic friction against infrastructure abuse.

---

## Incentive Alignment

The second purpose is to align operators with the long-term success of the network.

When operators hold stake, they are incentivized to:

* Maintain uptime
* Install software updates
* Report issues honestly
* Support ecosystem growth
* Operate reliable infrastructure

This creates a healthier and more resilient network.

---

## Accessibility

At the public sale price of $0.02 per QOR:

* 1,000 QOR = $20

This makes participation accessible while still preserving the economic protections provided by staking.

---

## Conclusion

The 1000 QOR requirement is not designed as a barrier to entry.

Instead, it serves as a lightweight security mechanism that protects the network from abuse while encouraging responsible infrastructure participation.

By combining low-cost access with economic alignment, QoreChain aims to make light node participation both accessible and sustainable.
